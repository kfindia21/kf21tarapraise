Honour Tara

The Living Principle Within & Around Us

Whether Tara is a name for something luminous in yourself, a quality you aspire to embody, or a goddess you hold dear — you are welcome here.

This is not about worship. It is a gentle, daily act of turning inward — honouring the wise, compassionate, and courageous feminine that lives in each of us.

Who is Tara?

Tara is one of the most beloved and cross-cultural embodiments of the Divine Feminine. In Hinduism, she is the fierce and protective Mother — the second of the ten Mahavidyas. In Buddhism, she is the mother of liberation, widely revered for protecting and saving beings from both spiritual and worldly suffering.

For us in the Tara Club, she is a living principle — not a distant deity, but a quality of awareness, tenderness, and fearless action that we are each invited to recognise and grow within ourselves.

✦

At the outer level

We honour Tara — praising her qualities, offering gratitude, and sending goodwill out into the world.

❧

At the inner level

We live her — recognising Tara's qualities within ourselves and letting them guide our everyday life.

The 21 Taras — 21 facets of the awakened heart

In Mahayana tradition, Tara manifests in 21 distinct forms — each a different expression of enlightened action: swift protection, fearlessness, abundance, compassion, wisdom, peace, and more. Whether understood as sacred deities or as archetypes of the awakened heart, together they paint a complete picture of the feminine principle in full bloom.
